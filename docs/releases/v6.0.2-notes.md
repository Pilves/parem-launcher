# Parem Launcher v6.0.2

A small update to 6.0.1. Requires Android 10 or newer.

Parem is written by AI coding agents under Patrick's direction.

- An app uninstalled from the drawer leaves the list as soon as the uninstall
  finishes. Some phones kept showing it until a restart.
- Dragging down in an app picker (folders, focus mode, quiet list and app
  limits) scrolls the list back up instead of closing the sheet.
- Private Space can be found by searching its name. Tap it or press Enter to
  unlock or open it; Android's hide-when-locked setting is respected.
- Apps on home whose launcher entry has changed open again in their own
  profile.

The first two came from closed-test reports. Thank you.

This APK is the full build, which can pair over wireless debugging on the phone
to grant grayscale. The Google Play build grants grayscale from a computer only.

## Updating

Install over your existing Parem installation without uninstalling it or
clearing its data. Exporting settings first gives you a backup.
