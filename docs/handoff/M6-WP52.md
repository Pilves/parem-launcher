Screen-time freshness checks treat future timestamps as stale after a clock rollback.
Contract: the home timestamp refreshes at one minute; the shared usage cache expires after one minute or on day change.
Not verified: manual or network time changes on physical devices and the Android usage-event contents across a clock change.
Easy to get wrong: invalidate both the persisted home throttle and the shared in-memory cache, including same-day rollbacks.
