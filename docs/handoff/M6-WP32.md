A disposable-emulator-only fixture exercises a synthetic notification's seeded quiet-list snooze and release through allowing its package and turning Quiet off.
Contract: the default instrumentation suite skips the fixture unless explicitly enabled; the separate workflow supplies that argument on a fresh emulator and restores listener access.
Not verified: automatic filtering of an eligible launcher app, real notifications, hardware/OEM differences, reboot or the eight-hour expiry.
Easy to get wrong: the shell notification is deliberately exempt and its snooze is seeded by the test; this is not evidence that positive filtering works.
