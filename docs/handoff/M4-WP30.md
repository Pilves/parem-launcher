Folders have "Edit folder" (name, apps and websites prefilled) and a folder made from an occupied slot starts with that slot's app or website; same-name widgets of one app show their cell size in the picker; debug builds are labelled "Parem Debug"; removing or replacing a widget prunes its height, and every restore pass prunes leftovers.
Contract: `CreateFolderDialog(context, apps, initial, onSave)` keeps a saved app that the picker doesn't offer (hidden apps), and an `initial` with an empty name still reads as creating; the rest of the row was already fixed: whole-row tap targets by M4-WP35, the drawer "Close" overlap by M4-WP53, and today's total by the shared `getPerAppUsageToday` map behind both home and the graph.
Not verified: the time pickers and the app-count picker are split into the device pass, since both are visual and no build here renders them; by reading, `TimePickerDialog(activity)` already takes the platform overlay on the app theme, and the picker background is opaque with weighted columns, so neither was changed. Folder editing and the widget label are unproven on a device.
Easy to get wrong: `targetCellWidth` throws below Android 12 and `minSdk` is 29, so it stays behind the version check in `sizedLabel`.
