Instrumented permission-loss HOME checks verify the actual launcher state, and a timed-out Play run cannot overlap Full or performance phases.
Contract: one HOME injection must reach resumed Parem with its home layout visible; a Play timeout fails the job and leaves Full and performance unverified.
Not verified: physical devices and whether Android test-services provider failures have other causes.
Easy to get wrong: the timeout does not prove leftover device instrumentation terminated; do not start another phase against it.
