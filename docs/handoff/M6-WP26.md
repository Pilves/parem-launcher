Smoke CI bounds each test/performance phase and diagnostic adb command, and stops later phases if the emulator is unavailable after failure.
Contract: any failed or timed-out phase fails the job; a reachable emulator still runs remaining checks, including the unchanged performance gate.
Verified: node harness tests simulate offline and online failures plus a failed stress-run clear; shell syntax and handoff checks pass.
Not verified: the bounds on a real CI emulator or whether they prevent Android's independent task-removal/reattach race; no Gradle or device used.
Easy to get wrong: don't turn skipped phases into a pass or remove orchestrator isolation; reattachment needs a separately measured fix.
