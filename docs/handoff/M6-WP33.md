A separate CI-only launcher app supplies synthetic notifications for positive Quiet filtering and release checks on a disposable emulator.
Contract: the fixture APK and its notification permission are independent of both shipping flavors; the normal suite does not enable the test without an explicit argument.
Not verified: real notifications, OEM behavior, reboot, full disclosure flow or the eight-hour expiry.
Easy to get wrong: emulator fixture results cannot promise notification delivery or release on every device.
