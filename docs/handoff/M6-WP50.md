Single-app auto-launch protects quick-action prefixes and settings prefixes unless the matched app label or a label word starts with the query.
Contract: all nonempty supported action prefixes are protected; settings prefixes yield to an app label/word prefix, including for hidden settings. Visible suggestions still require three characters.
Not verified: real-device typing, accessibility input and app lists beyond the CI emulator.
Easy to get wrong: a colliding setting is reached by tapping its row or through Settings; preserve both launch checks, explicit Enter, composition and leading-space web search.
