Opening the app drawer or Settings dismisses its respective first-run home tip, including accessible navigation routes.
Contract: acknowledge drawer guidance only after successful launch-mode drawer navigation; app-picking flows retain it; entering Settings acknowledges long-press guidance.
Not verified: spoken TalkBack, physical gestures, every navigation route and device restoration; CI and device checks are recorded in the PR.
Easy to get wrong: these acknowledgements stay device-local; a failed navigation or an app-picker selection must not dismiss the drawer tip.
