Default date, clock and Digital Wellbeing launches use the same Focus and app-limit decisions as other app routes.
Contract: the resolved handler is checked and pinned; Focus fails closed if the handler cannot be determined, while camera gestures and documented system actions remain exempt.
Not verified: the phone and tablet remain reserved; spoken accessibility and real-device intent handlers need a later pass.
Easy to get wrong: `gateLaunch` previously relied on the app callback for the Focus check; an unpinned chooser under Focus could open a blocked handler.
