The 7-day screen-time graph exposes each day column as a virtual accessibility node (ExploreByTouchHelper): TalkBack reads "Mon, 2 hours 18 minutes" (today suffixed), a D-pad moves a drawn focus ring between columns and Enter/Centre toggles the selection the same way a tap does; dimmed text in the graph, the sheet captions and the onboarding "Not now" use a new 60% tint that gives 5.74:1 on the light sheet (was 4.0–4.1:1).
Contract: `?attr/primaryColorTrans60` (`blackTrans60` #99000000 light, `whiteTrans60` #99FFFFFF night) is the secondary-text tint; `helper/ScreenTimeGraphText` owns the bar label, spoken-duration split and column hit-test, with a JVM test; `ScreenTimeGraphView.onDaySelected` is unchanged and fires for touch, TalkBack click and keyboard alike.
Not verified: Before integration, no device run, so TalkBack phrasing, keyboard focus traversal inside the bottom sheet and the focus ring's look are untested; the ratios are computed (sRGB blend on #FFF/#000), not measured on a screen.
Easy to get wrong: `primaryColorTrans50` is still used in 44 other places and sits below 4.5:1 in the light theme; new secondary text should use Trans60, and `setData` must call `touchHelper.invalidateRoot()` or TalkBack keeps stale day nodes.
Verified: ADB UI hierarchy on Nothing A069 / Android 16 exposes all seven graph days with spoken-duration descriptions; actual TalkBack speech and measured display contrast remain unverified.
