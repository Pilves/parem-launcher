Typing "alarm 7:30", "timer 10m", "event friday 3pm dentist" or "remind me 5pm call mum" (Estonian: "äratus", "taimer", "kohtumine"/"sündmus", "tuleta meelde") shows a quick-action tip; Enter or a tap sets it through `AlarmClock` intents (`EXTRA_SKIP_UI`) or opens the calendar editor prefilled; the empty drawer rotates omnibox example hints.
Contract: `helper/QuickActionParser.parse(query, now)` returns `Alarm | Timer | Event`, keyword-first, whole query must match, with must-parse and must-not-parse tables in the test; "remind me" with only a time is a labelled alarm, with a day a calendar event (DECISIONS.md); `isActionPrefix(query)` suppresses auto-launch on keyword prefixes of 3+ characters (`ala`, `alarm `, `remind m`) through `autoLaunchGuard`; the mode sits after web and before contact in `OmniboxResolver`; no new permission (SET_ALARM was already declared).
Not verified: OEM clock apps honouring `EXTRA_SKIP_UI`/`EXTRA_MESSAGE`; calendar apps returning to Parem after Save during focus; the Estonian keywords await Patric's review.
Easy to get wrong: quick actions bypass focus and the mindful pause by design (a quick action is not an app launch); the parser refuses to guess am/pm, so the tip preview is the user's only chance to catch 07:30 vs 19:30; relative times ("in 20 min") and filler ("set a timer") deliberately don't parse.
