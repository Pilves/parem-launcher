Today's screen-time scan keeps one `EventLogWrapper` and its unmatched-close guardian across cache misses; the guardian's 24 h lookback is grouped by package (`usageStats/GuardianLookback.kt`) and capped at two lookbacks per guardian.
Contract: `ScreenTimeFreshness.guardianReusable` sends a scan in cold on a new day or zone, a clock step over 1 s, now before the last scan, a non-Available read, a package-stamp change, or a guardian made in the first 5 minutes of the day; a cold scan is exactly the pre-WP74 scan.
Each scan logs one `ParemUsageScan` line (events, lookback, wallMs, cpuMs, guardian kept/new, gateCaller, gateBlocked, ok) in release builds, for the PERF-4b tablet gate; `readToday` is the gated entry, so only launch gates may call it.
Not verified on a device: the scan times, parity with a cold scan, and the clock, zone, access, reboot, midnight and uninstall edges.
Easy to get wrong: removing the timing log is about 15 lines across three files (counters included), not one.
