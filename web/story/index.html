A fork, and
what followed.

Parem is directed by Patrick. AI coding agents wrote the changes since the fork.

Parem began as a fork of Olauncher, created by tanujnotes and its contributors. Their work is the foundation; Parem remains open source under GPLv3.

Patrick directs what goes in and what gets cut. The result is a home screen with a clock, a short list and a search field that answers when you ask.

What you can check

The source, tests and CI runs are public.

Parem has no subscription or account. Some optional features make network requests: weather uses Open-Meteo, currency uses ECB rates, and optional wallpapers come from a list and image hosts. Services receive connection details.
